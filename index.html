2026

EpiDetect

Seizure Detection from EEG Signals

This one sits closer to research. I trained a 40 model ensemble (five random seeds across eight classifiers) on the Bonn University EEG dataset and got it to 99.4% accuracy with only three wrong predictions out of 500 test samples. The feature pipeline pulls out 720 dimensions covering spectral bands, Hjorth parameters, and wavelet coefficients, then runs union based feature selection with stratified cross validation to keep things honest.

Python scikit-learn Flask Signal Processing Ensemble ML NumPy
